The Crutch Test: dialog explorer

Real students given unrestricted GPT-4 scored 17% lower on a later exam taken without AI (Bastani et al., PNAS 2025). AI tutors are trained against simulated students, so a simulator used as a reward must show the same loss. This pilot tests one Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct student on 50 Big-Math problems: tutored by Claude Haiku 5.5 in three arms, then tested without help on a new variant of each problem.

  • Frozen simulators get the sign wrong. Answers raise their test score by +0.06 to +0.15, with 95% CIs above 0; real students lost 0.054.
  • Keeping more of the dialog means a larger false gain: +0.315 with the full dialog in view, +0.122 with the student's own turns, +0.060 with one short note.
  • A student that learns in its weights removes the gain (−0.022 and −0.003 at learning rates 0.1 and 0.03) but does not reproduce the human loss.
